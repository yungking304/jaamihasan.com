PRIVACY POLICY

Last updated: July 2026

THE SHORT VERSION

The Phantom Grid does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. Everything stays on your device.

DATA COLLECTION

The Phantom Grid collects no personal information. The app does not require an account, does not connect to the internet, and does not transmit any data to external servers.

LOCAL STORAGE

The app stores the following data locally on your device using Apple's UserDefaults system: your 3-letter leaderboard initials, your high scores and level progress, and your settings preferences. This data never leaves your device and is deleted when you uninstall the app.

THIRD PARTY SERVICES

The Phantom Grid does not use any third party analytics, advertising, or tracking services.

CHILDREN

The Phantom Grid is rated 4+ and is appropriate for all ages. We do not knowingly collect any information from children or any other users.
