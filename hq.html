Business HQ

Business HQ is Jay Hasan's private dashboard for running his businesses (The Candle, ImpressivelyDigital and Cerulane). Among other things, it schedules The Candle's daily lesson videos and uploads them to The Candle's own YouTube channel.

Uses YouTube API Services

It is not available to the public. Sign-in is limited to its owner.
